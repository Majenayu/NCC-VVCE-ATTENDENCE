
# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the NCC VVCE Attendance project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior includes:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the NCC community

**Unacceptable behavior includes:**
- Harassment, trolling, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information
- Unprofessional conduct

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer.
